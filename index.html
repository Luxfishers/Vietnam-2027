Long Tan, Sat 13 March

The battle

When and where
18 August 1966, in a rubber plantation near Long Tan, Phuoc Tuy Province (now Ba Ria–Vung Tau)
Who
108 ANZACs: 105 men of D Company, 6RAR, and 3 New Zealand artillery personnel
Against
An estimated 1,500 to 2,500 Viet Cong and North Vietnamese Army troops
Commander
Major Harry Smith

D Company fought for more than three hours in a heavy tropical downpour. They held on thanks to almost constant artillery fire from the Nui Dat base, an ammunition drop by RAAF helicopters, and the APCs that reached them at dusk.

Eighteen Australians were killed and 24 wounded. At least 245 enemy dead were counted on the battlefield. 18 August is now marked each year in Australia as Vietnam Veterans' Day.

More: DVA Anzac Portal and Australian Army – The Cove.

Our day

A full-day tour from Saigon is about 2 to 2.5 hours' drive each way. Typical stops:

  • The Long Tan Cross memorial in the plantation
  • Nui Dat, the former 1st Australian Task Force base
  • The Long Phuoc tunnels
  • Optionally Vung Tau

The plan is a private tour for the four of us, with site permits included, at about US$150 each.
